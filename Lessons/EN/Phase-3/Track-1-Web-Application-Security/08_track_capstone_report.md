# Track 1 · Stage 1.8 — Capstone Assessment Report

## Deliverable
Full assessment of **one** intentional lab application:

1. Scope and rules of engagement (lab only)
2. Methodology (mapping, accounts used, tools)
3. Findings table (title, severity rationale, evidence, remediation)
4. Positive notes / strengths
5. Retest plan
6. Cleanup (snapshots restored)

## Quality bar
A reader should reproduce your steps on the same lab app and understand risk without hype.
